\section*{Data and Code Availability}
The package at \url{https://github.com/alemaiorano/sim-to-real-validity} includes sources,
exporters, aggregate historical reports, the fixed panel, and original corpus manifests.
Reexporting these reports reproduces their displayed numbers, not their generation from raw
observations. Original reports are hash-pinned and unchanged. Separate corrected GPT derivatives,
input hashes, and offline replay verification are in \texttt{reanalysis/2026-10-31/}.
A separate replication bundle retains the recovered historical GPT scalar checkpoint, exact
exploratory inputs, and the new matched-budget experiment: all raw response bytes, request and
observation plans, immutable journal snapshots, coverage, package metrics, and source history.
Committed new aggregate reports and hash provenance are in \texttt{recollection/2026-10-31/}.
Offline raw replay needs no provider account. Historical Gemini observations remain missing; new
generations do not restore them. \texttt{REPLICATION.md} distinguishes these evidence tiers.
This is a local revision and artifact prepared for deposition, not a claim that this bundle is
already available at the public repository URL.

\section*{Ethics and Conflict of Interest}
The study uses public archive data and synthetic personas; no new human subjects were recruited.
The author has a commercial interest in copy-simulation tools. This evaluates a study harness
rather than a specific product. Disclosure, archived adverse results, explicit limitations, and
available historical artifacts and the accompanying local bundle support scrutiny; an unverified preregistration chronology is not presented
as a safeguard.
