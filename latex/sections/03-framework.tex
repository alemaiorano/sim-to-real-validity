\section{Sim-to-Real Validity Framework}
A package contains variants with observed outcome rates and predicted scores
(Figure~\ref{fig:protocol}). Upworthy was designed for randomized traffic, but the archive authors document randomization
problems during part of 2013--2014~\citep{matias2024correction}. We report observed rate rankings;
they are not automatically causal headline effects. Grouping distinct headlines can also combine
image variants. MIND ranks different articles within a subcategory by clicks divided by logged
article appearances, pooling user-level recommendation logs. Reddit groups reposts by image and
subreddit, pools votes for identical titles, and uses upvotes divided by total upvotes plus
downvotes. This is a vote-approval ratio, not CTR; vote totals are not exposure counts. Timing,
recommendation, and exposure remain uncontrolled in these observational comparisons.

\paragraph{Metric identity.}
For concordant and discordant pair counts $C,D$, Goodman--Kruskal gamma is
\[
\gamma=(C-D)/(C+D),
\]
discarding pairs tied in either variable~\citep{goodman1954}. It is undefined when $C+D=0$ and
differs from Kendall $\tau_b$, which accounts for ties:
\[
\tau_b=\frac{C-D}{\sqrt{(C+D+T_x)(C+D+T_y)}},
\]
where $T_x,T_y$ count pairs tied only in the respective variable~\citep{kendall1945ties}.
Spearman uses average ranks~\citep{spearman1904}. Constant rank inputs yield undefined values.

The primary experiment uses uniform expected top-1 and bottom-1 credit:
$|S_{\mathrm{pred}}\cap S_{\mathrm{real}}|/|S_{\mathrm{pred}}|$ for the corresponding extreme
sets. Captured lift averages observed rates across tied predicted maxima, then scales distance
from the observed minimum by the observed range. Constant outcomes make lift undefined.
Supplementary aggregate-report top-1 results use the first maximum and therefore depend on variant order.
