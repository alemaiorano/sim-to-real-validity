\section{Persona Panel, Prompt, and Reproducibility Details}
\label{sec:appendix}
\paragraph{Fixed panel.}
The ten-persona JSON is the reproducible input. Demographic inspiration~\citep{holcomb2013newsuse}
is not a deterministic recipe for recreating behavioral descriptions. The unweighted panel is
not an estimated population sample.

\paragraph{First-person template.}
\begin{quote}\small
You are simulating a specific person reacting to a news headline on social media in 2014.\\
Person: \verb|{description}| (age \verb|{age_group}|, \verb|{gender}|, mainly on \verb|{platform}|,
news engagement: \verb|{news_engagement}|.)\\
Headline: \verb|"{headline}"|\\
As this exact person, how likely are you to click this headline? Respond ONLY with JSON:
\verb|{"click_intent":| \verb|<number between 0 and 1>}|.
\end{quote}
The primary panel condition uses this first-person template; the generic-reader condition uses
the baseline template below. Each is sent as a single user message, without a system message.
The exact templates, including spacing, quotation marks and implemented alternative framings,
are authoritative in the versioned collector. A prompt implementation alone does not
establish a completed experiment. The primary collection uses fixed 2014 framing for all
headlines, including those created outside that year; it is not a date-specific audience
reconstruction.

\paragraph{Generic-reader baseline template.}
\begin{quote}\small
You are a typical U.S. social-media news reader in 2014.\\
Headline: \verb|"{headline}"|\\
How likely are you to click this headline? Respond ONLY with JSON:
\verb|{"click_intent":| \verb|<number between 0 and 1>}|.
\end{quote}
Both templates retain the U.S./2014 framing. The baseline specifies a generic reader role;
its distinction from the panel is the absence of the fixed demographic profiles, not the
absence of all role conditioning. Literal spacing, persona fields, and request prompts are
retained in the collector and raw request plans. The interpolated persona fields are description,
age group, gender, platform, and news engagement, all shown in Table~\ref{tab:persona_panel}.
Political lean is stored metadata and is not separately interpolated.

\paragraph{Coverage and checkpoints.}
Coverage policies differ by analysis. The GPT scalar-checkpoint analysis requires at least
one recorded score for every variant, with means over available observations. The primary
matched-budget experiment requires all planned scores in both arms for a retained package. Terminal failures,
request/choice indices, and exclusions are preserved. Configuration and source hashes prevent
mixing protocols. Each Azure request has one seed shared by its three returned choices; there
is no invented per-choice seed. GPT scalar-checkpoint collection requests did not send a seed.

\paragraph{Raw artifact and replay.}
The primary collector appends and fsyncs a request record before dispatch and retains the exact
response bytes, status, usage, version, and parsing outcome afterward. Hash-chained journals
are synchronously mirrored. Unknown outcomes after interruption remain unknown and are not
silently regenerated. Offline analysis validates the request plan, input hashes, response hashes,
and journal chain, then reparses the same immutable snapshot whose hash identifies the report.
The replication bundle contains that snapshot, all inputs, request plans, aggregate reports,
source history, and the retained GPT scalar checkpoint. Replaying saved bytes
requires no provider account and does not assert that future generations will match.

\paragraph{Supplementary outcome construction.}
MIND aggregates the supplied news and behavior logs by article ID, counting appearances in
recommendation lists and click labels across users. Articles need at least 200 appearances;
within each subcategory, at most ten are retained by appearance count, with at least two per
package. Reddit aggregates identical titles within image/subreddit pairs, requires at least
300 total votes per title, and retains at most ten titles by vote count. Upvotes are the
numerator and total upvotes plus downvotes the denominator, regardless of the schema's
\texttt{clicks}/\texttt{impressions} names. These inputs cover the supplied logs, not a matched
exposure window or common audience. Original manifests and the pinned corpus builders specify
the transformations; missing evaluated IDs prevent fully reconciling the supplementary samples.
