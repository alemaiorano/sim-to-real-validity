\section{Related Work}
Profile-conditioned models can approximate some aggregate survey distributions
\citep{argyleSiliconSampling}; broader computational social science applications raise validity
questions~\citep{ziems2024css}. Interview-grounded agents reproduce General Social Survey responses
at approximately 85\% of participants' own test--retest consistency in the original version of
\citet{park2024agents}. That result uses rich individual grounding and a different outcome from clicks.

Other work documents option-order sensitivity~\citep{dominguezolmedo2024questioning}, limited effects
of persona attributes on subjective annotation~\citep{hu2024persona}, persona-related degradation
on objective tasks~\citep{zheng2024helpful}, caricature~\citep{cheng2023compost}, and biased outcomes
from generated personas~\citep{li2025personacatch}. Reasoning biases in persona-assigned models are also documented~\citep{gupta2024bias}. These motivate a baseline, but do not explain
this study's gap.

Upworthy~\citep{matiasUpworthy} supports engagement analyses including negativity effects
\citep{robertson2023negativity}. LOLA~\citep{ye2024lola} studies LLM-assisted content experiments;
differences in selection, models, and evaluation prevent a direct numerical comparison here.
MIND~\citep{wu2020mind} and Reddit reposts~\citep{lakkaraju2013name} are additional observational
settings, not interchangeable headline experiments. Construct validity~\citep{cronbach1955construct}
distinguishes intent from behavior. Gamma~\citep{goodman1954} must also be distinguished from
Kendall correlation and its treatment of ties~\citep{kendall1938,kendall1945ties}.
