\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
\paragraph{Corpus.}
Upworthy rows are grouped by experiment and distinct headline, aggregating identical text.
Packages require at least two headlines and $3{,}000$ impressions per headline; sampling uncertainty
matters in online experiments~\citep{kohavi2009controlled}. The locked exploratory corpus has
\sensNVariants{} variants in \sensNPackages{} packages. Original manifests remain unchanged.

\paragraph{Historical winner screen.}
The original procedure chose the observed best variant and compared it with the observed runner-up
using a one-sided pooled two-proportion $z$ test at $p<0.05$, selecting \legacySelectedN{} packages.
The direction was chosen from the data: even a two-arm null comparison can pass in either
direction, so the nominal one-sided threshold does not imply a five-percent selection error.
Comparing only the runner-up need not separate a winner from every opponent. Outcome selection
changes the evaluation population. Packages failing a screen are not thereby unpredictable.

\paragraph{Corrected screen.}
The revised rule requires a unique observed maximum and compares it with every opponent using
two-sided pooled $z$ tests. For $k$ variants the comparison family contains $m=k(k-1)/2$ unordered
pairs. All winner--opponent comparisons must pass $\alpha/m$, with $\alpha=0.05$.
The screening statistic is $\min(1,m\max_{j\ne w}p_{wj})$. Bonferroni's inequality motivates the
adjustment~\citep{nistBonferroni}. Error control is approximate and per package, conditional on
independent binomial observations and adequate normal approximation. It is not an across-package
false-discovery guarantee or proof of a population winner. Repeated exposures and Reddit voting
further limit these assumptions. In the restored corpus \newSelectedN{} packages pass, all within
the historical selected set. A stricter screen does not demonstrate that removed packages had
false winners.

\paragraph{Estimation and denominators.}
Package metrics are averaged without size weighting. Corrected reports include
$n_{\mathrm{total}},n_{\mathrm{valid}},n_{\mathrm{undefined}}$ and metric-specific package IDs.
Each paired gap uses packages valid in both arms for that metric. Empty estimates are undefined,
not zero. Percentile bootstrap CIs use $10{,}000$ package resamples, seed $42$
\citep{efron1979bootstrap}. They condition on selection and recorded scores and omit generation,
selection, and outcome-measurement uncertainty. Historical coefficient-specific valid counts
are not fully recorded; coverage totals are not those denominators.

\paragraph{Planning and robustness scope.}
The archived power calculation is an earlier planning artifact. Fisher-$z$ power for one
correlation does not justify an average of within-package coefficients, and Cohen's conventions
\citep{cohen1988power} do not automatically calibrate gamma. This revision makes no prospective
power or equivalence claim. Time-stamped preregistration of all thresholds is not established.

Historical robustness artifacts cover three Upworthy splits, MIND~\citep{wu2020mind},
Reddit~\citep{lakkaraju2013name}, three Gemini models, seed runs, and aggregation. No identifiable
result artifact supports the previous prompt-robustness claim. Reddit's manifest records many
more selected packages than the evaluated subset; its subset-selection procedure is undocumented.
