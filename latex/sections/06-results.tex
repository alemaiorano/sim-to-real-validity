\paragraph{Primary matched-budget comparison (RQ1).}
Tables~\ref{tab:new_gemini} and \ref{tab:new_azure} give the primary matched-budget experiment.
Supplementary comparisons below retain their own protocols and evidence boundaries. The complete-package policy retains
\newGeminiN{} Gemini packages and \newGptN{} GPT-4.1 packages; exclusions are respectively
\newGeminiExcludedN{} and \newGptExcludedN{}. Reports retain every planned observation and
failure, with metric-specific valid and paired IDs.

For Gemini, panel and baseline Kendall $\tau_b$ are \newGeminiPanelTau{} and
\newGeminiBaselineTau{}; the paired baseline-minus-panel gap is \newGeminiGapTau{}
(CI [\newGeminiGapTauLow{}, \newGeminiGapTauHigh{}]) on \newGeminiPairedTauN{} valid pairs.
Expected top-1 is \newGeminiPanelTopOne{} versus \newGeminiBaselineTopOne{}, with paired gap
\newGeminiGapTopOne{} percentage points (CI
[\newGeminiGapTopOneLow{}, \newGeminiGapTopOneHigh{}]).

For GPT-4.1, panel and baseline Kendall $\tau_b$ are \newGptPanelTau{} and
\newGptBaselineTau{}; the paired gap is \newGptGapTau{}
(CI [\newGptGapTauLow{}, \newGptGapTauHigh{}]) on \newGptPairedTauN{} valid pairs.
Expected top-1 is \newGptPanelTopOne{} versus \newGptBaselineTopOne{}, with paired gap
\newGptGapTopOne{} percentage points (CI [\newGptGapTopOneLow{}, \newGptGapTopOneHigh{}]).
In Gemini, $\tau_b$ is undefined on \newGeminiBaselineRankUndefinedN{} baseline packages
and \newGeminiPanelRankUndefinedN{} panel packages. Marginal means therefore use different valid
sets; their difference is not the paired gap. The paired rank comparison uses their valid
intersection, while top-1 includes every complete package with expected credit for ties.
Baseline-minus-panel CIs exclude zero for the rank metrics, top-1 and captured lift in both
configurations; bottom-1 CIs include zero. These are pointwise conditional estimates.
Gamma and $\tau_b$ have distinct rows and denominators; neither is substituted for the other.
These conditional package-bootstrap intervals omit repeated-generation uncertainty and do not
compare model families causally. The GPT scalar-checkpoint analysis uses a separate collection protocol.

\windowGeminiPairedTauN{} valid pairs; its tau-b and Spearman intervals include zero.

is not robust across this cohort restriction and all rank estimators. Gamma remains a distinct

include zero for both models. These are post-hoc, pointwise intervals, not an equivalence test.

\paragraph{Signal strength (RQ2).}
The pinned supplementary producer defines its separation index as $1-p$, where $p$ is the
one-sided observed-best-versus-runner-up value, and correlates it with the panel's first-index
top-1 hit. The stored Spearman association is \hThreeTopOne{} (CI
[\hThreeTopOneCILow{}, \hThreeTopOneCIHigh{}]) over \hThreeN{} packages, using $5{,}000$
bootstrap resamples. Its code requires every variant to have a score and within-package
Spearman to be defined, even for this top-1 analysis. The report alone cannot identify which
excluded packages lacked scores or had undefined ranks. This index mixes outcome separation
with exposure, and top-1 difficulty also varies with the number of variants; the association
is unadjusted for those factors. It is not evidence of an intrinsic predictability effect.
Missing supplementary predictions prevent recomputing the analysis under simultaneous screening
and expected tie credit; the primary selected-cohort experiment does not estimate this
all-package association.

\paragraph{Dataset comparisons (RQ3).}
Table~\ref{tab:replication} shows higher baseline gamma on the three Upworthy splits and a smaller
difference on MIND, where packages compare articles. On Reddit, the panel has higher top-1
(\redditPanelTop{} versus \redditBaseTop{}) and lift (\redditPanelLift{} versus \redditBaseLift{}).
The available summaries show different directions, but unreconciled collection and subset
differences prevent attributing this variation to the dataset alone. Reddit gamma
CIs include zero; this establishes neither equivalence nor absence of a title-related signal.
Posting time and visibility were not tested as explanations. Subset-selection and coverage
limitations further restrict interpretation. The aggregate summary does not record draw
counts or evaluated package IDs, so cross-dataset protocol equivalence is not independently
verified by those aggregates.

\paragraph{Seed, model, ties, and aggregation.}
The supplementary three-seed SDs (\stabGammaSD{} for gamma; \stabTopOneSD{} for top-1) use the
population convention over those seeds, not sample SDs or precise general-variability estimates.
The baseline has higher gamma on three tested Gemini tiers (Table~\ref{tab:cross_model_gap},
Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix}); evaluated IDs are not retained to verify a shared subset.
This cannot establish universal model or prompt robustness.

The tie report rounds scores before counting constant packages; the rank code uses exact
equality. That count cannot recover excluded gamma values. Undefined coefficients cannot be
silently assigned zero, and the reports do not justify a conservative-lower-bound claim for the gap.

Table~\ref{tab:h5agg} preserves six supplementary aggregation rules, all with lower reported gamma
than the baseline. Equal-persona and observation-weighted means can differ with missing draws.
The reports do not establish why their top-1 results differ, or whether bias, sampling noise,
scale, or another factor explains relative performance.

\paragraph{GPT-4.1 scalar-checkpoint analysis.}
The recovered checkpoint contains scalars, not original responses. Offline replay of the pinned
producer reproduces all published cross-family report fields within numerical tolerance.
Collection coverage is \xfN{} panel and \xfPairedN{} baseline/paired packages.
Thirty panel observations versus ten baseline draws and no generation seed make it
an unequal-budget comparison.

Table~\ref{tab:corrected_gpt} applies simultaneous screening and tie-aware estimators to those scores.
Coverage becomes \correctedPanelN{} panel, \correctedBaseN{} baseline, and \correctedPairedN{}
paired packages. Baseline Kendall $\tau_b$ is \correctedBaseTau{} versus \correctedPanelTau{}.
Paired CIs for the displayed gaps exclude zero, supporting a positive baseline gap in this
checkpoint analysis. It is not an independent replication or a budget-matched estimate, and does not validate
raw parsing. Gamma and tau-b remain separate. The supplementary Gemini observations are unavailable for applying these estimators; the
primary Gemini comparison uses its own retained generations and configuration.
