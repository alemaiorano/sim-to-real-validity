\section{Conclusion}
This fixed demographic persona panel should be compared with a generic-reader LLM baseline.
Historical Upworthy results favor that baseline, whereas Reddit top-1 and lift favor the panel.
An offline GPT-4.1 diagnostic retains a positive paired baseline gap, conditional on recorded
post-parser scores and unequal budgets.

The original coefficient was gamma, not Kendall tau. Selection and undefined denominators also
required correction. We release distinct metrics, expected tie credit, explicit valid and paired
counts, and an adjusted winner screen. These improve the protocol without establishing universal
persona harm or a causal mechanism. Complete corrected historical Gemini analysis requires
the missing observations.
