\section{Conclusion}
This fixed demographic persona panel should be compared with a generic-reader LLM baseline.
The new raw-retained experiment favors the baseline on valid rank comparisons, expected top-1
and captured lift in both tested configurations. For expected top-1, baseline-minus-panel gaps
are \newGeminiGapTopOne{} percentage points for Gemini and \newGptGapTopOne{} for GPT-4.1.
Bottom-1 intervals include zero; a blanket advantage across metrics is not established.
Historical Reddit top-1 and lift favor the panel.

The original coefficient was gamma, not Kendall tau. Selection and undefined denominators also
required correction. We now release distinct metrics, expected tie credit, explicit valid and
paired counts, an adjusted winner screen, and an offline raw-replay artifact for the new
collection. Rank estimates condition on valid coefficients, and all intervals condition on
selected packages and recorded generations. The new data do not restore lost historical
Gemini observations, establish a general causal mechanism, or show universal persona harm.
