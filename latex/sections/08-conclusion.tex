\section{Conclusion}
This fixed demographic persona panel should be compared with a generic-reader LLM baseline.
The primary new raw-retained comparison favors the baseline on valid rank comparisons, expected
top-1 and captured lift in both tested configurations. In the post-hoc sensitivity excluding the
archive's documented randomization-risk period, top-1 and lift gaps retain positive pointwise
intervals, but Gemini tau-b and Spearman intervals include zero. The rank advantage is therefore
not uniformly supported across these cohorts and estimators. In the primary comparison, expected top-1 baseline-minus-panel gaps
are \newGeminiGapTopOne{} percentage points for Gemini and \newGptGapTopOne{} for GPT-4.1.
Bottom-1 intervals include zero; a blanket advantage across metrics is not established.
Historical Reddit top-1 and lift favor the panel.

The comparison uses distinct rank metrics, expected tie credit, and explicit valid and paired counts.
We provide a simultaneous winner screen and an offline raw-replay artifact in the accompanying local
replication bundle, prepared for deposition. Rank estimates condition on valid coefficients, and all intervals condition on
selected packages and recorded generations. Historical Gemini observations remain unavailable. The new experiment neither establishes
a general causal mechanism nor shows universal persona harm.
