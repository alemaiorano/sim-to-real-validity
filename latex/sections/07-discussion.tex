\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
The comparisons make a generic-reader baseline necessary for this task. A persona panel adds
assumptions about audience composition and behavior that require predictive validation.
The new matched-score comparisons favor the baseline in valid rank comparisons, expected top-1
and captured lift, but do not establish a bottom-1 advantage. Gemini rank means exclude constant
outputs with explicit arm-specific counts; top-1 retains those packages through expected tie
credit. Historical Upworthy reports and the scalar GPT diagnostic also favor the baseline;
historical Reddit decision metrics favor the panel. Excluding the documented randomization-risk
period preserves the new top-1 and lift contrasts but leaves Gemini tau-b and Spearman uncertain.
The ranking conclusion therefore depends on cohort and estimator, not just model configuration.

\paragraph{Mechanism remains open.}
The baseline may use general headline knowledge, while role-play may add unhelpful conditioning.
Neither explanation was isolated experimentally. Similar score spreads, selected error examples,
and rank-only aggregation failures cannot distinguish bias from noise or identify a mechanism.
The new balanced, interleaved collection tests the panel/baseline comparison with retained raw
responses, but does not separately manipulate demographic content, role-play, or response noise.
It therefore does not by itself identify those mechanisms.
The archive measures clicks on Upworthy's website, while the prompt frames a social-media
reader in 2014 and omits the image. This platform/time framing is not an exact reconstruction
of the test audience. Intent versus engagement remains a construct-validity limitation~\citep{cronbach1955construct}.

\paragraph{Ground truth and generalization.}
The Upworthy correction documents a period of potentially invalid randomization
\citep{matias2024correction}; our selected cohort includes affected-window tests. Our predictions
rank recorded outcome rates, and neither an adjusted screen nor new model responses establishes
causal headline effects. The post-hoc exclusion sensitivity addresses one known risk window,
not every possible allocation or image confound. The corrected screen addresses selection under approximate binomial tests; it does not make
outcomes error-free. Exclusion changes the target population and relative task difficulty.
Failure to reject equality does not imply no predictor can recover an ordering.
MIND and Reddit are observational; correlated exposures, time, image, audience mix, and topic
may matter. Training contamination and generalization to current audiences or other formats
are not excluded by a historical corpus.

\paragraph{Reproduction limits.}
Aggregate reports allow reexport, not complete verification of their generating process.
Historical Gemini observations and some historical report producers were not recovered. The GPT checkpoint
supports replay and partial reanalysis, but range-checking stored scalars cannot retroactively
validate the permissive parser. The new strict-parser observations have complete raw lineage and matched within-model score
budgets; they are not mixed with legacy scores. Azure multi-choice and Gemini single-choice
requests differ operationally. Returned aliases are not immutable model snapshots, and request
seeds do not guarantee determinism. A single collection session does not estimate provider drift
or generation variability. Content filtering can cause exclusions associated with content or arm;
the Azure technical pause also divides its collection in time. The historically selected cohort limits generalization.
Prompt robustness, deterministic demographic-to-panel construction, full Reddit subset lineage,
and preregistration chronology remain evidence gaps.

Preserving historical artifacts and separating corrected derivatives prevents numerical
verification from being mistaken for empirical revalidation.
