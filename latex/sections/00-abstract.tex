\begin{abstract}
Do demographic personas improve an LLM's prediction of audience engagement? We compare a fixed
ten-persona panel with a generic-reader baseline on Upworthy headlines, with historical news and
Reddit comparisons. An artifact audit found that the original ``Kendall tau'' was Goodman--Kruskal
gamma, winner screening selected its direction from observed outcomes, and some conclusions exceeded
the reports. We preserve historical results with corrected labels, provide a retrospective GPT-4.1
scalar-checkpoint diagnostic, and collect new Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite and GPT-4.1 observations on the
same \newSelectedN{}-package corrected exploratory cohort. The new experiment matches thirty scores
per arm and variant within model, retains exact raw responses, and discloses metric-specific valid
and paired counts. Primary complete-package coverage is \newGeminiN{} for Gemini and \newGptN{} for GPT-4.1. Its baseline-minus-panel Kendall $\tau_b$ gap is \newGeminiGapTau{}
(CI [\newGeminiGapTauLow{}, \newGeminiGapTauHigh{}]) for Gemini and \newGptGapTau{}
(CI [\newGptGapTauLow{}, \newGptGapTauHigh{}]) for GPT-4.1, on respectively
\newGeminiPairedTauN{} and \newGptPairedTauN{} valid rank pairs. Expected top-1 is
\newGeminiPanelTopOne{} versus \newGeminiBaselineTopOne{} for Gemini and \newGptPanelTopOne{}
versus \newGptBaselineTopOne{} for GPT-4.1 (panel versus baseline). These intervals condition on the
selected cohort and recorded generations. A newly incorporated archive correction documents a
randomization-risk period. Excluding that period in a post-hoc sensitivity retains positive
expected top-1 and lift gaps in both configurations, while Gemini tau-b and Spearman intervals
include zero. Historical Reddit top-1 and captured lift favor the
panel, contradicting a prior blanket baseline-superiority claim. The new collection supplies a
raw-replay artifact, not recovered historical Gemini scores, a new held-out population, or evidence
of universal persona harm. Provider request protocols differ across models, and no causal mechanism
is isolated.
\end{abstract}
